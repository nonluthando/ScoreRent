How much rent you can realistically afford

Affordability is one of the strongest signals in a rental application.

Why affordability matters

Landlords and agents usually want to see that rent is manageable compared with income or funding. A listing can be attractive but still risky if rent takes too much of your monthly income.

ScoreRent budget bands

  • Conservative: around 25% of monthly income.
  • Recommended: around 33% of monthly income.
  • Upper limit: around 38% of monthly income.
  • High risk: around 45% or more of monthly income.

Remember upfront costs

Rent is not the only cost. Deposits, application fees, transport, moving costs, and first-month expenses can make a listing difficult even when rent looks acceptable.

A lower-rent listing with complete documents is often stronger than a stretch listing with weak affordability.
