How to improve a weak rental application

Focus on the biggest weakness first instead of applying everywhere.

Common ways to improve

  • Add missing required documents.
  • Use a guarantor if income or funding is not enough.
  • Target listings closer to your recommended rent band.
  • Avoid high-demand areas if your application is already borderline.
  • Prepare documents before applying so you can respond quickly.

What to fix first

If ScoreRent flags affordability, start with rent, income, funding, or guarantor support. If it flags documents, do not apply until you can add the missing documents or explain the gap clearly.

The goal is not to apply everywhere. The goal is to avoid paying application fees for listings where your chance is weak.
