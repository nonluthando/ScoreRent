What documents landlords usually ask for

Most rental applications are judged partly on how complete and clear your documents are.

For workers

  • Recent payslip.
  • Three months of bank statements.
  • Employment letter or confirmation of employment.
  • Copy of ID or passport.

For new professionals

  • Signed employment contract.
  • Offer letter.
  • Bank statement if available.
  • Guarantor support if income history is limited.

For students

  • Proof of registration.
  • Student card or student number.
  • Bursary, NSFAS, or funding confirmation if funded.
  • Guarantor letter, guarantor payslip, and guarantor bank statements if not funded.

Before applying, compare your documents against the listing requirements. A good application can still look weak if one required document is missing.
