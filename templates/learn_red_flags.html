Rental red flags before paying an application fee

A strong ScoreRent result does not automatically mean the listing is safe.

Warning signs

  • The landlord or agent refuses a viewing.
  • You are pressured to pay immediately.
  • The rent is far below similar listings in the area.
  • The person avoids written details or a proper lease process.
  • You are asked to send money before confirming the property exists.
  • The photos look copied, inconsistent, or too polished for the price.

Before you pay

  • Verify the property address.
  • Ask for clear written terms.
  • Check the agent or company identity.
  • Avoid sending documents to suspicious contacts.

Treat ScoreRent as application guidance, not fraud verification. Always check the listing and person you are dealing with separately.
